## Sources

Third-party sources are cited references, paraphrased rather than reproduced.
The linked routing, request-receipt and image/timeline schemas are original synthesis.

- **S1:** BYU Family History Library, Julia A. Anderson,
  [U.S. Military Pension Files (1776–1916): A Gold Mine](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OcAeLJxT9XU),
  15 July 2024. Service clues, index versus file, requests and chronological reading.
- **S2:** BlackProGen LIVE!,
  [Episode 65: Civil War Pensions and the Grand Army of the Republic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nSfAqzYaiWM),
  15 August 2018. Application/certificate locators, unsuccessful applications and reading the
  continuing claim. Historical transfer assertions are not adopted as current custody rules.
- **S3:** Ancestry, Loretta Evans,
  [U.S. Military Pension Files](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RRzzQVNQU2I),
  20 April 2016. Separate service, pension and bounty-land records; unsuccessful applications.
- **H1:** National Archives,
  [Requesting Copies of Older Military Service Records](https://www.archives.gov/veterans/military-service-records/pre-ww-1-records),
  page reviewed 16 January 2026; checked 7 October 2026. Request forms, federal scope and
  complete-file versus Pension Documents Packet products.
- **H2:** National Archives,
  [Genealogy Research in Military Records](https://www.archives.gov/research/military/genealogy),
  checked 7 October 2026. Record-type, service category and custodian routing.
