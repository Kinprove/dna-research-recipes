## Sources

The prose, audit matrices, local labels and handoff are original synthesis.
Sources are cited for attribution and methodological support; their text, model
citations and certification rubrics are not reproduced. Checked 2026-10-07.

- Board for Certification of Genealogists, [Ethics and Standards](https://bcgcertification.org/ethics-standards)
  — GPS, documentation and separation of source information from commentary.
  Rights: cited for attribution, not reproduced.
- BCG, [Reporting Research in Progress](https://www.bcgcertification.org/skillbuilding-reporting-research-in-progress)
  — bounded objectives, reproducible findings and reporting limitations.
  Rights: cited for attribution, not reproduced.
- BCG, [Reasonably Exhaustive: How Do We Know We Are There?](https://www.bcgcertification.org/ten-minute-methodology-reasonably-exhaustive-how-do-we-know-were-there)
  — question-specific assessment of research breadth and remaining evidence.
  Rights: cited for attribution, not reproduced.
- BCG, [Proof Statements 1](https://www.bcgcertification.org/ten-minute-methodology-proof-statements-1)
  and [Proof Summaries and Arguments 1](https://www.bcgcertification.org/ten-minute-methodology-proof-summaries-and-arguments-1)
  — context-dependent proof statements and the continuum of writing forms.
  Rights: cited for attribution, not reproduced.
- Elizabeth Shown Mills, [QuickLesson 19: Layered Citations](https://www.evidenceexplained.com/content/quicklesson-19-layered-citations-work-layered-clothing)
  and [Source Citations: The Velcro Principle](https://www.evidenceexplained.com/quicktips/source-citations-velcro-principle)
  — consulted versions, source-of-source details and locator ownership.
  Rights: cited for attribution, not reproduced.
- Mills, [Negative Evidence vs. Negative Findings](https://www.evidenceexplained.com/quicktips/negative-evidence-vs-negative-findings)
  — absence observations and justified evidentiary interpretation.
  Rights: cited for attribution, not reproduced.
- BCG, [Transcribing Source Materials](https://bcgcertification.org/skillbuilding-transcribing-source-materials)
  — distinguish faithful transcription and researcher additions.
  Rights: cited for attribution, not reproduced.
- National Genealogical Society, [Guidelines for Sound Genealogical Research](https://www.ngsgenealogy.org/guidelines_sound-research2016-final-30sep2018/)
  — source identification, hypothesis testing and qualification of conclusions.
  Rights: cited for attribution, not reproduced.
- Society of Genealogists, [Writing Genealogical Reports](https://sog.org.uk/guides-and-stories/writing-genealogical-reports/)
  — report and proof-summary purposes. Rights: cited for attribution, not reproduced.
- FamilySearch, [FamilySearch GEDCOM specification](https://gedcom.io/specifications/FamilySearchGEDCOMv7.html)
  — scope of technical data representation. Rights: cited for attribution, not reproduced.
- Nicole Elder Dyer and Diana Elder, [RLP 189: Revisiting Research Logs Again](https://familylocket.com/rlp-189-revisiting-research-logs-again/)
  (2022-02-21) — logs include negative searches and candidate/associate research
  beyond the subject's event timeline. Rights: cited for attribution, not reproduced.
- Dyer and Elder, [RLP 101: Proof Arguments — Certification Preparation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rI8F07ph5h8)
  (2020) — practical selection and organization of written proof.
  Rights: cited for attribution, not reproduced.
- Dyer and Elder, [RLP 193: Five Takeaways from the DNA Proof Arguments SLIG Course](https://familylocket.com/rlp-193-five-takeaways-from-the-dna-proof-arguments-slig-course/)
  (2022-03-21) — organize proof around reasoning and distinguish it from reporting
  the research process. Rights: cited for attribution, not reproduced.
