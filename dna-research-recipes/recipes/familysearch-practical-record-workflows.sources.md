### Source qualifications

- S01's camera indicates the image viewer; its paper/star icon identifies Full Text in the
  demonstrated interface. Its extracted release chronology is inconsistent with its spoken
  February 2024 date, so no release chronology is used here.
- S01 and S02 disagree about availability of a transcript-editing control. Workflow 10 preserves
  the corrected reading locally and does not depend on that control.
- S02 describes PID-only Simple Search as untested; that suggestion is excluded.
- S14's indexed-record/image-count arithmetic mixes units and is excluded. S13 demonstrates an
  entry labeled 100% indexed after describing Images broadly as unindexed; status is group-specific,
  and that label alone does not establish Full Text availability.
- S09's complete-group total is 1,138. Its later “830 of 158” is inconsistent; workflow 7 keeps
  item and complete-group numbering separate.
- S10 and S11 credit Virginia Pratt in their bodies. S08's saved Pros/Cons sections and referenced
  screenshots are incomplete; their missing contents do not supply any recipe step.
